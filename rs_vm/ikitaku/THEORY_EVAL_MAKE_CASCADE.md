## 5. Cascade vs collapse

| Notion | Where it lives | Meaning |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| **Cascade** | `l6_cascade`, `upward_cascade`, state/charge cascade, method cascade | Try next model / escalate layer / propagate state |
| **Collapse** | curve flatten, residual→0, circle halt, remainder irreducible, narrow_pct | Space or uncertainty reduces to a usable unit (or to “cannot”) |
| **Approach curvature** | `goal_approach` / `goal_curve` | Progressive gap close (fractional collapse of joint error) |
| **Construct narrowing** | pos/neg filter | Collapse of *candidate method* set before assemble |

**Theory reading (consistent):**
- **Cascade** = ordered expansion of attention (more models, up the ladder, into open bags).
- **Collapse** = ordered reduction of ambiguity (constraints, curve flatten, accept synthesis, close bag).
- Healthy solve alternates: cascade when stuck / unsure layer; collapse when pos/neg + evidence allow.

**Not the same as** “always cascade” or “always Occam collapse.” Occam is a *preferred collapse* under low residual; desperation *cascades* models.

**Verdict:** Implicitly practiced in several modules; **not yet one named dual operator**. Worth treating as first-class: every construct/solve step is either cascade (open search) or collapse (commit/narrow).

- Layer conf never correlates with later residual → meta-conf theater.
