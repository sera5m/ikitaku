## Schema crack: comparison as first-class

Comparison is not “are these the same costume.” It is:

1. cluster by **axis** (magnitude, polarity, purity, rate, span)
2. fuzzy Hegel **on the comparison** (thesis=pole, antithesis=other pole, leftover=the axis)
3. Lie + associate on those attrs (the two engines)

`small` / `big` are not two R5 verbs. They are opposing **composition attributes** of `magnitude` — a super-set of threshold / fuzzy compare. Adjectives modify the bag (what). Adverbs modify the transform (how). Both live at R3–R5 as antiderivatives of a platonic axis.
